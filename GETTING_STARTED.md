# Start here

Read README.md for the scientific question and reproduction commands.

1. **Explore:** pick a named measurement and sol. The graph updates immediately. Red points are review candidates, not proven faults.
2. **Relationships:** compare raw and adjusted correlations, then inspect PCA. Variables must be aligned measurements, not anonymous channels stacked arbitrarily.
3. **Data:** inspect source examples, quality exclusions, units, and archive links.
4. **Methods:** review aggregation, baseline, score, threshold, and limitations.
5. **Notebooks:** read saved outputs or download the three notebooks and rerun them with Python.

The current study covers ten Martian sols. It does not predict failures or reconstruct the rover’s route.
