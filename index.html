Units and meaning

Air temperatures are local measurements at each boom; they may include rover effects. Ground temperature is a radiometric brightness temperature, not a universal surface temperature. Celsius = Kelvin − 273.15. Pressure is in pascals. A sol is a Martian day; LMST is local mean solar time. Unknown and −999 values become missing, never zero.

HOW THE STUDY WORKS

Understand first. Flag second.

01 · Read and validate

Download ten original PDS tables and their detached labels. Parse fixed-width columns from MODRDR6.FMT, validate file length and row count, and record SHA-256 hashes. Reject duplicate sol/timestamp pairs.

02 · Apply quality rules

Air: reliability bit 7 must be 1. Ground: power, recalibration, stationary rover and no-shadow bits 0–3 must all be 1. Pressure: no-shadow bit 1 must be 1; bit 0 identifies the oscillator and is not treated as a quality failure. These are explicit study choices, not a guarantee that every retained value is perfect.

03 · Aggregate without inventing data

Median in 10-minute LMST bins, at least 10 accepted observations per measure. No gap filling or interpolation. The median daily profile gives equal weight to each available sol, rather than each original observation.

04 · Review unusual observations

For each local-time bin, subtract its median across the ten sols (at least five contributing sols). Compute s = |r − median(r)| / (1.4826 × MAD(r)). Scores above 3.5 are candidates for review; 3.0 and 4.5 show sensitivity.

This is exploratory and uses the same dataset to describe and flag. There is no train/test split, no assumed 1% anomaly rate and no claim that 3.5 guarantees a false-alarm probability. Missing or zero-scale scores remain unavailable.

05 · Compare relationships

Show correlations before and after removing the time-of-day median. PCA uses complete bins, four standardized variables and sample standard deviations. Components come from SVD; the circle uses variable–component correlations. Short arrows are poorly represented in the first two components.

Limits that matter

Ten sols cannot establish seasonal patterns. Sampling is uneven; quality filtering changes coverage. Temporal dependence makes adjacent bins non-independent. Shared daily cycles are not evidence of causation. The same-data daily profile may mask persistent departures. Flagged bins have no ground-truth failure labels and are never described as confirmed faults. The 3D rover illustrates hardware; it does not reconstruct a drive or sensor position from the data.

What happened to Isolation Forest?

The earlier anonymized-telemetry benchmark is preserved in version history. This study answers a different question using named physical measurements and descriptive statistics. Machine learning is not required for this question.
