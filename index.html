SYNTHETIC EXPERIMENT

Two perspectives on a simulated signal.

Statistical baseline

A robust median/MAD score detects unusual values in four causal features. Its threshold is set using a synthetic calibration segment.

Isolation Forest

A machine learning model with 100 trees scores observations that are easier to isolate. Its scores and metrics are computed only on generated data.

What this demo means

Reference intervals were intentionally inserted into simulated data. Metrics here are not the research project's historical benchmark and do not establish operational reliability.

WELCOME TO ROVERWATCH

Your first exploration.

  1. Choose a channel and a method. Results update automatically when your selection changes.
  2. Read the signal. Red points mark detections; shaded regions show synthetic reference intervals.
  3. Inspect an alert. Click to zoom into its context, then reset to see the full signal.
  4. Compare methods. Precision: the share of detections that are correct. Recall: the share of anomalous samples detected.
  5. Explore Curiosity. Drag the rover, zoom in, and select its components.

The data is synthetic and generated. The 3D model is an educational illustration, not a reconstruction of mission events.
